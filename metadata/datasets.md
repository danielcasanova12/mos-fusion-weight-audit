# Dataset provenance and redistribution

No audio, listener-level data, rating-level records, or embeddings are included
in this repository. Obtain every corpus from its original distributor and read
the current terms before use.

| Corpus | Role in this study | Official provenance / access note |
|---|---|---|
| BRSpeechMOS | Brazilian Portuguese synthetic speech | Introduced with the BSpeech MOS prediction work. Access links and preparation code are maintained by the original authors: <https://github.com/freds0/BSpeech-MOS-Prediction>. The upstream repository should be consulted for current terms; this artifact grants no data license. |
| BVCC | English TTS and voice conversion | VoiceMOS Challenge 2022 resource: <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.6572572>. The release states that Blizzard Challenge samples may not be redistributed and supplies acquisition/preparation instructions instead. Upstream components retain their own terms. |
| SingMOS | Singing synthesis and conversion | VoiceMOS Challenge 2024 resource page: <https://sites.google.com/view/voicemos-challenge/resources> and dataset paper <https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10911>. Follow the distributor's current terms. |
| TMHINT-QI | Mandarin clean, noisy, and enhanced speech | Introduced by the InQSS/TMHINT-QI work: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.02585> and distributed through the VoiceMOS Challenge resource page. Follow the distributor's current terms. |

The committed CSVs contain aggregate experiment outputs and system-disjoint
selection summaries only. They are not substitutes for the upstream datasets.
